# 👗 The Thread Affair – Women's Fashion Boutique | Inventory & Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

**The Thread Affair** is a data analytics project focused on analysing inventory management and sales performance for a small women's fashion boutique.

The project explores how inventory and sales data can help identify slow-moving products, potential overstock, and money tied up in unsold inventory.

The analysis is based on a simulated boutique dataset, designed around realistic small-retail operations and women's clothing categories.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Excess inventory and unsold clothing can significantly affect a boutique's profitability and cash flow.

This project aims to answer important business questions:

- Which products are selling the most and the least?
- Which clothing categories generate the highest revenue?
- How much inventory is currently available?
- Which products have high remaining stock?
- Which products have low purchase sell-through?
- How much money is tied up in potential overstock?

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall inventory and sales performance.
- Identify best-selling and least-selling products.
- Evaluate category-wise sales performance.
- Calculate current stock and inventory value.
- Identify potential overstock and low sell-through products.
- Present actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The project uses four interconnected datasets:

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| Products | Product details, categories, sizes, colours and pricing |
| Opening Inventory | Initial stock quantities and stock values |
| Purchases | Purchase transactions, suppliers, quantities and costs |
| Sales | Sales transactions, quantities, discounts and revenue |

**Product categories include:**
- Kurtis
- Dresses
- Ethnic Suits
- Winter Wear
- Festive Wear
- Traditional Stitched Wear
- Women's Tops

*Note: The dataset is synthetically generated for learning and portfolio purposes. It does not contain actual customer or business transaction records.*

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Excel Analysis
- Data cleaning and preparation.
- Data model relationships.
- KPI calculations.
- Best-selling and least-selling product analysis.

- Missing value and duplicate checks.
- Inventory calculations.
- Product-wise and category-wise sales analysis.
- Potential overstock analysis.
- Purchase sell-through calculations.
- Data validation.
- Exporting analysis results into CSV files.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis is designed to help a boutique owner:

- Understand which clothing categories perform better.
- Identify products that require closer inventory monitoring.
- Reduce unnecessary purchasing of slow-moving products.
- Monitor the value of unsold inventory.
- Make more informed purchasing and stock-management decisions.

*Actual findings and numerical insights will be added after reviewing the final analysis results.*

## 📈 Business Value

This project demonstrates how data analytics can support small retail businesses in making better inventory decisions.

By combining sales and purchase information with current stock calculations, boutique owners can identify potential inventory risks and make more informed decisions about future purchases.
